Full Rules

14 Rules

  1. A minimum of 7 and a maximum of 12 games are picked each week.

  2. TWO games each week are the required games of the week — everyone must pick these. They are Monday Night's NFL game and the College GameDay game.

  3. Of your 7–12 picks, at least 3 must be college and 3 must be NFL (the required games count toward these). So at a minimum you pick 2 of each on your own, plus the two required games — and you must still hit the 7-game minimum overall.

  4. Each week you may designate one game as a DOUBLER, worth double (win or loss). Pick all 12 games with a doubler and you can effectively win or lose 13.

    The doubler doesn't change the 7-game minimum — you still need at least 7 separate games, with the option to make one of them the doubler.

  5. A week consists of games from Thursday through Monday.

  6. The contest runs from September 10th, 2026 through December 7th, 2026 (13 weeks).

  7. The Foote Rule. Each game can only be picked once — you cannot pick both sides and guarantee yourself a .500 record.

  8. The official spreads are posted Thursday morning on the Entry Website. You'll get an email — and a text, if you opt in — the moment the new week's lines are up.

  9. Picks are due by midnight ET on Friday. Once that deadline passes, everyone's picks become available to view. You cannot pick a game after it has started.

    Picking a Thursday game? It locks the moment it kicks off — before Friday's deadline. When you lock in a Thursday pick, you must commit on the Entry Website the number of games you'll pick that week and your doubler status.

  10. If your picks aren't in by midnight ET Friday, you're given the default picks: the favored team in the first 2 college games Saturday, the favored team in the first 3 NFL games Sunday, and the favored teams in both required games.

  11. The player with the best winning percentage collects the winnings, calculated as:

    ( wins + (ties ÷ 2) ) ÷ ( wins + losses + ties )

    There are three payouts: a weekly winner, an overall percentage winner, and a most-wins-for-the-season winner. The tiebreaker is total games picked; if still tied, the money is split evenly.

    No declaring defaults this year. You either submit your own picks or you're given the defaults — there's no in-between. If you're given the defaults, you are not eligible for any weekly awards that week, though your record still counts toward the season-long (YTD) standings.

  12. The remaining money is paid out to the overall winners (roughly):

    1stOverall percentage winner~50%
    2ndRunner-up~25%
    3rdThird place~10%
    4thFourth place~5%
    BonusMost wins picked for the entire year — roughly 10% of the pot.
    RefundWorst winning % gets at least their money back.

    Percentages are approximate and adjusted to the size of the pot.
